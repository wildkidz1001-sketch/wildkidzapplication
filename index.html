04 // Safety & Governance

TERMS & CONDITIONS

Clear, transparent standards designed to protect young performers and ensure a fair global audition.

Auditions are strictly open to young performers aged 14 to 16 at the time of application. Proof of age and formal parental/guardian verification will be required prior to participating in live online callback rounds.
Every applicant must obtain explicit awareness and permission from a parent or legal guardian before submitting an audition. All official communication regarding callbacks, schedules, and agreements is conducted through the designated adult-managed guardian contact.
WILDKIDZ treats all applicant data with strict confidentiality. Your personal details, audition videos, and guardian contact information are never sold, published publicly, or shared with unauthorized third parties. All audition communications are supervised through secure, adult-managed channels.
Submitted videos must feature the applicant performing live vocals, rap, or choreography without heavy vocal pitch-correction or misleading edits. WILDKIDZ maintains a zero-tolerance policy toward bullying, discrimination, or disrespectful behavior throughout the audition process.
