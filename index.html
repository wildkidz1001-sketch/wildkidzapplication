About WILDKIDZ (WK)

WILDKIDZ (WK) is a global youth talent collective built to discover, develop, and spotlight emerging performers in music, dance, and live performance.

Audition Categories & Requirements

Vocal

1–2 minute clear singing clip without heavy background vocals or vocal effects.

Dance

1–2 minute full-body choreography or freestyle clip in a well-lit space.

Rap

1–2 minute original or cover verse demonstrating rhythm, flow, and delivery.

All-Rounder

Combined showcase highlighting at least two skills (vocal, dance, or rap).
